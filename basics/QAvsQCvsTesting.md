- Testing: Find defects in the software.
- QC: Check whether the product meets quality requirements.
- QA: Prevent defects by improving the development process.

1. Comparison table

| Aspect | Testing | Quality Control (QC) | Quality Assurance (QA) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | Executing software to find defects and verify its behavior. | Checking whether the product meets specified quality standards. | Ensuring that processes are followed to produce quality software. |
| **Main focus** | Software functionality and behavior. | Product quality. | Development and testing processes. |
| **Main goal** | Find defects. | Identify defects and ensure requirements are met. | Prevent defects from occurring. |
| **Approach** | Primarily product-focused. | Product-focused. | Process-focused. |
| **Nature** | A set of verification activities. | A reactive activity that identifies existing quality problems. | A proactive activity that reduces the likelihood of defects. |
| **When performed** | During development and after implementation, as appropriate. | Throughout development, wherever product checks are needed. | Throughout the software development lifecycle. |
| **Who is involved?** | Testers and developers. | Testers, inspectors, and other quality personnel. | The whole development team, including QA professionals and management. |
| **Examples** | Running test cases, checking login functionality, API testing. | Checking whether the application meets acceptance criteria and required standards. | Defining testing standards, reviewing processes, and conducting process audits. |

ex: Developing a university student registration system.

**Testing — Find defects**
- A tester enters an incorrect password and discovers that the system allows the user to log in.
- The activity reveals a software defect.

**Quality Control (QC) — Check product quality**
- The team checks whether registration, login, and student record functions meet the specified requirements.
- The activity evaluates the quality of the actual product.

**Quality Assurance (QA) — Improve the process**
- The team establishes coding standards, code review procedures, and testing guidelines to reduce the chance of similar defects in future development.
- The activity improves the process used to build the product.
